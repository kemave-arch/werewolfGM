Roles & Signals Guide

Seer vs. Oracle: • Seer (🔮): Inspects one living player directly. GM signals 👍 (Wolf) or 👎 (Innocent). • Oracle (🧿): Points to two adjacent neighbors to inquire if at least one is a Werewolf, or asks about fallen spirits!
Twins (♊): Night 1 only: The Twins wake together, make eye contact, and vouch for each other's innocence throughout the match.
Bodyguard / Protector (🛡️): Shields one player from attack. Cannot pick the same target two consecutive nights!
Witch (🧪): Shows tonight's victim. Witch nods for Heal, points for Poison.
Hunter (🏹): When dying, immediately chooses one player to drag into the grave with them.
Tanner / Fool (🎭): Wins solo if executed by the village during the day vote!
